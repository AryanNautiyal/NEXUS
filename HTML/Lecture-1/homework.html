Biography of Nikola Tesla

Early Life

Nikola Tesla was born on July 10, 1856, in Smiljan, Croatia (then part of the Austro-Hungarian Empire). He was the son of Milutin Tesla, a Serbian Orthodox priest, and Georgina Đuka Tesla, who was known for her inventiveness.

Career

Tesla studied engineering at the Technical University at Graz and later at the University of Prague. He worked as an electrical engineer in Europe before moving to the United States in 1884.

Achievements

Tesla is best known for his contributions to the development of alternating current (AC) electrical systems, the Tesla coil, and wireless transmission of energy. He held over 300 patents for his inventions.

Legacy

Tesla spent his later years in New York, continuing to work on new inventions. He died on January 7, 1943. Today, he is remembered as one of the most influential inventors and visionaries in the field of electricity and magnetism.
